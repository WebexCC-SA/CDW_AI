# Let's Cheat!

> In this lab you will be using Real-Time Assist to help you figure out the mystery person in the game of Guess Who.


## Call In And Play
> Launch the agent desktop
> 
> Use the Agent Desktop to make an outbound call to 14849925078
>
> Open the AI Assistant
>
> The AI Assistant will give you suggestions of characteristics to ask about to help narrow down the mystery person.
>
> The AI Assistant will also give you Names to guess once you have narrowed down enough information.
